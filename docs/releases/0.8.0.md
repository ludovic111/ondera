# Ondera 0.8.0

## Recording
- Input monitoring: hear the microphone through the track's inserts, sends and fader. The button
  beside Arm cycles Off, Auto (while armed, until the track plays its own clip, and again while
  recording) and On; also in the mixer, the inspector, the Track menu and on `I`.
- One input stream serves the meter, monitoring and the take, so what you hear no longer drops out
  when a take starts, and the meter keeps moving through the count-in.
- The built-in microphone into the built-in speakers would howl, so that pairing stays muted
  until you choose Monitor Anyway. Headphones are not affected.
- Settings > Audio > Buffer Frames sets the device buffer (64 to 2048). `audio.status` reports the
  measured input and output buffers, the frames waiting between them and the resulting latency.
- The status line shows "Count-in…" during the count and lets go of a finished MIDI take.

## MIDI controllers
- Your keyboard's mod wheel, pitch bend, sustain pedal, expression and other controllers play the
  instrument live and are recorded into MIDI takes.
- MIDI regions keep them: they play back on the exact sample, pick up the right values when you
  start in the middle of a region, and never leave a note bent or held after Stop or at the end of
  a region.
- Controller lane under the piano roll (View > Controller Lane, `L`): pick Mod Wheel, Expression,
  Sustain, Pitch Bend, Pressure, Volume, Pan, Breath or any CC, then click to add a point, drag to
  move it, draw a curve, and Option-click or right-click to delete.
- Built-in instruments answer pitch bend (±2 semitones), the mod wheel and pressure (vibrato) and
  the sustain pedal. CLAP, VST3 and Audio Unit instruments receive controllers too.
- Split, trim, duplicate, paste and reverse keep controllers in step; MIDI files import and export
  them.

## Arrangement
- Audio regions fade in and out: drag the handles at a region's top corners, or type the lengths
  in the inspector. Equal power, linear or exponential curves.
- Region gain from −60 to +24 dB in the inspector; the waveform shows what you will hear.
- Splitting, trimming and copying a region keep its fades sensible. Exports and stems play them
  exactly as the window does.
- Markers name the song's sections on the ruler. ⇧M adds one at the playhead, ⇧B and ⇧N jump
  between them, ⇧C loops the section you are in. Drag a marker to move it, double-click it to
  rename it, right-click it for more.
- Plugin automation lands on its sample: a breakpoint in the middle of a buffer takes effect on
  that sample, and ramps are sent every 32 samples, for CLAP, VST3, native and stock plugins.
  Audio Units and older native plugins are processed in short slices so they follow too.
- Changing the time signature keeps automation on its bars.

## Files
- Ogg Vorbis export for mixes and stems, with a quality setting from Small (about 96 kbit/s) to
  Maximum (about 500 kbit/s), High (about 192 kbit/s) by default. The file carries the song's
  title, and the report shows the bitrate.
- FLAC export, 16 and 24-bit, for mixes and stems. It streams: a twenty-minute song grows the
  heap by about 2 MiB. Stems take the chosen file type instead of always being WAV.
- A mix path with an extension Ondera does not write (`.mp3`) is refused instead of quietly
  holding WAV. There is still no MP3 export: no MP3 encoder fits the licence and the build.
- Audio import skips damaged frames instead of refusing the file, trims the MP3/AAC lead-in,
  reads the sound of video files and names the file in its errors.
- File > Import MIDI and Export MIDI work in the window. Saved songs and exports keep normal file
  permissions and write through symlinks.

## Plugins
- Native plugin ABI 2: controllers, pitch bend and pressure, parameter changes on their sample,
  state of the plugin's own, a tail length and latency-change notices. ABI 1 plugins keep loading
  and new plugins still load in older Ondera versions. The `Plugin` trait is source-compatible.
- One browser row per plugin instead of one per channel layout ("(m)", "(s)", "(m->s)", and the
  VST3 "Mono" / "Stereo"); the row loads the layout a stereo track wants.
- Plugins land in the folder their name describes, and every format of one plugin shares a folder:
  Other Effects is empty on a library of about 2,250 plugins.

## CLI, MCP and agent
- 177 commands. New: `controller.list`, `controller.add`, `controller.update`,
  `controller.remove`, `controller.setPoints`, `marker.list`, `marker.add`, `marker.rename`,
  `marker.move`, `marker.setColor`, `marker.remove`, `marker.goto`, `marker.next`,
  `marker.previous`, `marker.cycleSection`, `clip.setFades`, `clip.setGain`, `track.setMonitor`,
  `audio.allowSpeakerMonitoring`, `view.fit`, `clip.copy`, `clip.cut`, `clip.paste`,
  `agent.models`, `agent.connection`, `rhythm.preview`, `app.openGuide`. `transport.locate` takes
  a `markerId`; `session.exportAudio` and `session.exportStems` take `quality` for Ogg;
  `ui.showPanel` covers the command palette and the controller lane.
- The clipboard is shared: copy in the window, paste from the CLI, or the other way round.
- The agent's Changes list shows only edits from the agent, the CLI or MCP, and `session.batch`
  is one entry there, as it is one undo step.

## Fixes
- Musical typing works on any keyboard layout; ⌥ shortcuts work on macOS.
- Relaunch works without an update; update checks give up after a minute and clean up abandoned
  downloads.
- A broken `settings.json` is kept as `settings.json.invalid` instead of being replaced, so API
  keys are not lost.
- Bypassing an insert from a script no longer empties the slot; a failed batch no longer empties
  Redo; two tracks loaded from one file no longer share a plugin; names count characters, not
  bytes; presets whose names map to one file no longer overwrite each other.
- A tiny region no longer stops the arrangement painting; the grid follows 3/4 and other meters;
  3-, 5- and 7-channel files keep their centre in both ears.
- About thirty smaller fixes to undo steps, menus, context menus, error messages, the plugin
  window, forms, renames with an input method and quick double presses of Space.

## Site
- A "What's new" section and a changelog of every release, markers, fades and monitoring in the
  interactive preview, readable text in every theme, a section menu on phones, and fonts served
  by the site itself so the page makes no third-party requests.

## Known limits
- Monitoring, the shared input stream and the count-in are covered by tests that drive the input
  callback directly; they have not yet been tried with an audio interface.
- Two interfaces on different clocks drift: expect an occasional tick (counted in `audio.status`).
- Controllers reach a track's instrument, not its insert effects, on MIDI channel 1; polyphonic
  aftertouch is not recorded. CLAP instruments that only speak CLAP notes get no controllers.
- Stock effects do not smooth their parameters, so a fast automation ramp moves in 32-sample steps.
