# ryolune 0.11.1

Follows 0.11.0 (the rename, notarized macOS builds) a day later.

## Free forever, donations welcome

- ryolune is MIT licensed and free forever, every update included. Nothing is sold and nothing
  is locked: donations are the only money ryolune takes, and they are optional.
- Help > Support ryolune… and Settings > About > Support ryolune… open ryolune.com/support, where
  you can donate once or every month. They used to ask you to pay what you want, once, which read
  like a price.
- The window still asks one time only, after your third export, and never again; its button now
  reads Donate…. It counts exports in `settings.json` only; nothing is sent.
- `app.openGuide guide=support` says the same to the CLI, MCP clients and the agent.

## Site

- The site lives at ryolune.com. Every other address it answered on (www and the Railway one)
  now sends you to the same page there, and the app, the README and the repository's Sponsor
  button link to ryolune.com/support.

Limits from 0.11.0 still apply. See the
[0.11.0 notes](https://github.com/ludovic111/ryolune/blob/v0.11.1/docs/releases/0.11.0.md).
