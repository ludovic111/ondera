# ryolune 0.12.0

The window, redrawn, and an agent that works with more of the tools you use. ryolune had six
themes; it now has one, drawn with more care, in a dark and a light mode. The agent runs on many
more services, outside agents connect in one step, and sounds can be made from a description.

## One theme, dark or light

- **Dark** is deep graphite: displays sunk into the bar, white fader caps, controls with a fine
  top highlight and a dark edge instead of gloss or texture. **Light** is the same window in
  porcelain, with white controls standing proud of a pale desk.
- One accent, lunar gold (amber in light mode), marks the playhead, lit keys, focus and what the
  agent touched. Meters keep their own mint and amber.
- Settings > Interface shows Dark, Light and Auto (follows the system) as live miniatures.
- The themes of earlier versions (Modern, Skeuomorphic, Frutiger Aero, Console, Ink, Neon) open
  as ryolune in the mode they had. `interface.appearance` is now always `ryolune`; scripts and the
  agent choose with `interface.mode` (`dark`, `light` or `auto`). Setting an old theme name is
  refused with a message that says so.
- Text contrast is tested on every reading surface in both modes, as before.

## Details

- The transport display sits in the middle of the bar. Return, rewind and forward share one tray,
  play, stop, record and cycle another; play lights up in gold while the song runs, and the record
  key keeps a red dot.
- Knobs show their value as a ring around them. Pan fills from the top, so a track panned off
  centre shows at a glance; sends and plugin dials fill from the left stop.
- Mute lights blue, solo yellow and record arm red, in the track headers, the inspector and the
  mixer.
- Regions keep their track colour deeper and clearer, with a fine top edge.
- Thin rounded scrollbars, a soft gold focus ring, and dialogs with a calmer header. The main
  action of a dialog (Export…, Save) is lit.
- Fixed: the agent panel's status light was drawn as an empty square; the inspector's mute, solo
  and arm keys were cramped; the monitoring key no longer pushes the mixer's audio strips out of
  line.

## Sounds from a description

- The agent panel has a **Generate** tab: describe a loop, a song, a sound or an instrument and
  ryolune asks the service chosen in **Settings > Generation**: ElevenLabs (Eleven Music and its
  sound-effects model), Stable Audio by Stability AI, any fal.ai audio model, or your own HTTP
  endpoint. You pay the service directly; ryolune takes nothing.
- A loop follows the song's tempo and key and is trimmed or padded to exactly its bars. Songs and
  sounds land at the playhead as audio clips; an instrument lands on a new track in **Sample
  Keys**, playable from the keyboard. Each placement is one undo step.
- Every result is kept on this computer under **Your sounds**: listen, add it again, turn it into
  an instrument, or delete it.
- Commands: `generate.audio`, `generate.services`, `generate.list`, `generate.preview`,
  `generate.place`, `generate.delete`, so the agent, the CLI and MCP clients can do the same. The
  agent's new **Generate sounds** permission (on by default) covers it; agents can never change
  the generation service or its keys.

## Sample Keys

- A new stock instrument (the twelfth): it plays one sound across the keyboard from its root
  note, with Tune, Attack, Release, Gate or One-shot, Level, sustain pedal and pitch bend. The
  sound, up to 20 seconds, is saved inside the song.
- `strip.loadSample` turns any audio file, or audio already in the song, into Sample Keys on a
  new or chosen MIDI track.

## More agents

- The built-in agent now also runs on **Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral, Groq, DeepSeek and
  xAI** (an API key each, or the usual environment variable) and on **Ollama and LM Studio**
  running on this computer. Each service keeps its own key; the model field lists the models
  your key gives access to.
- **Settings > Agent > Use another agent** gives Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code, Claude
  Desktop, Gemini CLI, Windsurf, opencode, Zed and any other MCP app a ready connection to the
  open window: a command or a configuration block with this computer's paths, a Copy button, and
  one click for Cursor and VS Code. `agent.mcp` gives the same to the CLI.
- The panel is reorganised: a header with New conversation, settings and collapse; tabs **Chat,
  Generate, Changes, Takes**; your messages in bubbles; one composer card with the model picker
  and a round Send key. **Rhythm Lab left the panel**; `rhythm.create` and `rhythm.preview`
  remain commands for the agent and scripts.

## Sponsor

- A heart at the right of the title bar opens ryolune's GitHub Sponsors page (through
  ryolune.com/support). ryolune stays free and MIT licensed, every update included, and nothing
  is locked: sponsoring, once or monthly, is optional.
- ryolune.com/support now goes straight to GitHub Sponsors; it used to fall back to the page's own
  support section. The site has a Sponsor button in its navigation, and the repository shows
  GitHub's Sponsor button.
